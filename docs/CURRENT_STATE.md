## Next task

Do **not** jump directly into buying components.

Next milestone:

### Design Freeze v0.1

1. Draw external mechanical envelope.
2. Select realistic target overall size.
3. Convert visual proportions into approximate dimensions.
4. Draw the head retraction volume through Active/Sleep/Shutdown.
5. Draw wheel clearance and manual tuck envelope.
6. Draw arm swept volume and stow recess.
7. Draw battery service volume.
8. Draw internal functional zones.
9. Identify hard dimensional constraints.
10. Then begin component review **one component at a time**.

After each component is verified, feed its real physical dimensions and constraints back into the CAD envelope.

## Important reminder

No old PDF or `.ino` file has authority over the new design.

Legacy files can suggest ideas, but every relevant assumption must be revalidated.
